# Submission copy

ResolutionDuel is a reusable MIT-licensed GenLayer Intelligent Contract for comparing two frozen, evidence-backed market-resolution proposals. Independent validators rerun one strict semantic adjudication and consensus is stored as a closed decision, settlement outcome, and compact defect/uncertainty masks. It is not a market or custody contract. Downstream consumers pin chain/address/policy/fingerprint and use a fail-closed exact decision gate. Local lint/typecheck, 54 direct tests, and four five-validator GLSim scenarios pass. StudioNet and Bradbury deployments, canonical registrations, adjudications, exact deployed-source hashes, and exact/altered fingerprint gates are finalized and recorded. The audit receipt also discloses earlier non-canonical liveness failures that stored no state.
